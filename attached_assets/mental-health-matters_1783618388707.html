🌱 Mental Health Matters

Growing Healthy Minds, Together

Here at Lulubug's Garden, we believe that a lifetime of healthy minds is possible for all of us. Our adventures in the garden are all rooted in building skills that foster positive mental health from a very early age. Learning to recognize and address feelings, making decisions about how to deal with negative emotions, and learning how to support each other, are all part of why we created Lulubug's Garden.

We believe that mental health is part of our overall health. We also know that there are many people who do not know where to turn for mental health support and treatment. By partnering with two innovative groups, we believe we can share correct principles with a non-threatening, de-stigmatizing approach.

Huntsman Mental Health Foundation

Huntsman Mental Health Foundation is transforming how the world understands, treats, and advances mental health care through groundbreaking research, innovative care, and national leadership. Powering Huntsman Mental Health Institute as well as research-based efforts across the country, Huntsman Mental Health Foundation continues to innovate what philanthropy and community support can mean in diagnosing, researching, treating, and helping heal, mental health challenges of all kinds.

Lulubug's Garden is grateful to Huntsman Mental Health Foundation for helping provide the Mental Health Moment for each episode. To help be a part of this incredible mission of hope, reach out: Visit HMHF

Huntsman Mental Health Institute

The Huntsman Mental Health Institute was created to transform how we understand and treat mental illness. Built on a $150 million gift from the Huntsman family, the Institute is the first of its kind to bring research, education, and patient care together to solve one of our nation's greatest challenges: untreated mental health conditions.

Their caring team is there to help — with services ranging from individual therapy to specialized treatment programs. With research, programs, and treatments for all ages, from young children to teen and young adults all the way to grandparents, they are there to support everyone in their journey to healing.

Lulubug's good friend Dr. Karen is part of the exceptional team at Huntsman Mental Health Institute that provides world-class mental health care to children and their families. We are so glad that we can welcome experts like Dr. Karen to the garden as we all learn and grow together! Learn more about HMHI

You're Never Alone

We believe learning positive mental health skills is critical to preventing challenges as we grow. Sometimes, however, we may need some extra help. If you or a loved one is struggling or in crisis, contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. No-cost, confidential support is available nationwide, 24/7 by phone, text, or chat.
