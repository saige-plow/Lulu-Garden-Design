Meet the Friends Who Call the Garden Home

All feelings are okay — even the confusing ones. Lulubug and her friends learn to name their emotions and grow through play, music, and friendship, one adventure at a time.

Meet Our Garden Friends

Get to know the whimsical characters joining Lulubug on her next big adventure!

Lulubug

Lulubug is the heart of the Garden and often helps guide her friends through new experiences. She loves organizing, making lists, and drawing with the children who visit her garden. When things don't go as planned, she learns how to move from frustrated to brave and try again.

Curby — episode still

Curby

Curby is a fun, food-loving caterpillar who brings big energy to everything he does. He's curious, enthusiastic, and loves diving into whatever captures his imagination. Sometimes that excitement can turn into moments of carelessness or impatience when things don't go the way he expected.

Sterling — episode still
(looking at camera)

Sterling

Sterling is thoughtful and observant, often taking a moment to understand what's happening before jumping in. He likes to ask questions and think things through, helping the group see situations from different perspectives. When emotions start to feel big or confusing, Sterling's steady nature helps everyone slow down and make sense of what's going on.

Daisy — episode still

Daisy

Daisy loves to jump into every moment with excitement and laughter, bringing joy and energy to the group.

Willow — episode still

Willow

Willow brings a quieter presence, helping friends pause, listen, and understand their feelings.

Feelings Flower — episode still

Feelings Flower

The Feelings Flower helps kids recognize and name their emotions with colorful petals that represent different feelings. It's a fun way to learn that all emotions are okay — and that understanding how we feel is the first step to handling any feeling that comes.
