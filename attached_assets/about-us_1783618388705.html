Meet the Creator

Jil

Jil draws on her background in special education and her experience raising seven children to shape the heart of Lulubug's Garden.

After navigating her own wellness journey, she became dedicated to using art, music, and nature as tools for family connection.

As a creator of the show, Jil ensures every episode provides a gentle, supportive space for children to explore their emotional world.

Dr. Karen photo

Meet Dr. Karen

Dr. Karen

Dr. Karen is a board-certified child psychiatrist who brings deep expertise in child development to everything we create.

She reviews all our content to ensure it fosters emotional wellbeing and age-appropriate growth — giving families confidence that this is a space built with children in mind.
